Partnership process

A reviewed path from registration to coordination

  1. 01

    Register

    Submit organisation and contact information through the existing Staffing Partner registration flow.

  2. 02

    Review

    Aadhyant reviews the submitted profile and account information.

  3. 03

    Approval

    Approved partners receive portal access according to the existing account workflow.

  4. 04

    Requirement Assignment

    Aadhyant may assign suitable manpower requirements to an approved partner.

  5. 05

    Workforce Coordination

    The partner coordinates Candidates or workforce against requirements explicitly assigned to it.

Aadhyant coordination boundary

Access is purposeful, limited and assignment-based

Aadhyant remains the coordination layer between Employers, Candidates and Staffing Partners. Partner registration does not provide unrestricted access to Employer, Company, Candidate or administrative databases.

  • Only approved accounts can access the partner workspace.
  • Operational requirement access depends on an explicit assignment.
  • Private Employer, Candidate and administrative information remains protected by existing permissions.
  • Registration does not guarantee business, payment, requirements, Candidate volume or revenue.
