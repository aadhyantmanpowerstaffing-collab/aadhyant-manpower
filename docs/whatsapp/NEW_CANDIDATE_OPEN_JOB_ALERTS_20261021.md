# Open-job alerts for later registrations

Newly registered, consenting candidates can receive matching jobs published before registration. The minute wake and canonical automatic claim both prepare at most 100 missing recipients per invocation. No new candidate, application, campaign, outbox, or parallel delivery queue is created.

Preparation requires an authorized publication, an uncancelled registered-alert campaign, current public/open/unfilled eligibility, unchanged job snapshot, a resolved uniquely linked opted-in contact, and the correct independent general/Same-Day Pay choice. Candidate registration/contact/consent must postdate publication. Qualification, trade, age, experience and preferred location are matched from the saved profile; explicit preferences take precedence over current location. The structured qualification is authoritative, with controlled handling of education lists stored under Other, All Trade, and free-text Mechanical Diploma / Graduate Trainee. Unknown required age or trade does not match. Existing applications, excluded recipients, and any prior vacancy outbox row for the same job/person are not replayed.

Recipients join the existing canonical publication campaign. Completed or failed campaigns reopen only for newly included recipients; old outbound failures are not retried. Cancelled campaigns remain cancelled. The publication outcome retains its original cohort provenance; campaign and recipient delivery states show the later activity.

Template freshness is validated by the existing worker after metadata sync and again at the provider boundary. Preparation can create waiting recipients with stale metadata so that a completed campaign can wake the worker to refresh approval. STOP, changed profile, closed/filled jobs, changed snapshots, quota and template gates remain authoritative. The extra matching gate applies only to recipients tagged registration_catchup; existing publication recipients keep their current behavior. Same-Day Pay retains its separate preference, local eligibility and approved Hindi contract.

Migration installs functions only and does not enqueue an existing audience or invoke the provider. Production preflight observed three open authorized empty campaigns and zero candidates/eligible contacts. Runtime tests exercise later signup, exact current vacancy shapes, deduplication, consent, applications/exclusions, stale approval recovery, final-send profile changes, Same-Day Pay Hindi, the 100-recipient preparation cap, and shared daily quota. All provider calls are local fixtures.
