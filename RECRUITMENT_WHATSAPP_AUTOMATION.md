# Recruitment WhatsApp workflow

The Candidate portal records attending/unavailable against the exact interview schedule revision. A reschedule resets the response. It never changes a selection or joining outcome. Admin Follow-ups combines pending replies, unavailable candidates, attendance checks, recorded absences, and active joinings. A past schedule is an attendance check, not an automatically recorded absence.

Joining Message Details stores reporting time (IST), reporting location and candidate-facing document/instruction text. Existing private notes, employee codes, document contents and identity numbers are not copied into messages. The candidate can review these details in Joining Status.

Three transactional template contracts, each in English and Hindi, use six body variables: vacancy reference, role, event date, reporting time, venue and instructions. Exact specifications are under docs/whatsapp. Interview confirmation and reminder have Will attend / Cannot attend quick replies. The signed WANotifier webhook resolves these only against the original outgoing wamid, matching contact and current schedule revision; free text and guesses are ignored. Candidate portal confirmation works independently of WhatsApp activation.

The canonical whatsapp_outbound_messages queue is reused. Confirmation replaces legacy interview_schedule automation: preparation and final eligibility require interview_notifications_enabled=false. Confirmation is once per schedule revision. One reminder is eligible 30 minutes–24 hours before the interview, only after that revision's confirmation has been sent for at least one hour, and never for an unavailable response. Joining details require a future/today date and explicit reporting time and location. Relevant joining edits advance the notification revision. Every notification has one provider attempt; ambiguous receipt or persistence outcomes require reconciliation.

Deployment leaves recruitment_updates_enabled=false, recruitment_templates_verified_at=null, the Edge switches off and the scheduler installation lock intact. No candidate messages or backfill are issued by migration.

Activation requires all six exact templates approved and API Notifications saved; each generated private endpoint installed in the corresponding WANOTIFIER_INTERVIEW_CONFIRMATION_{EN,HI}_URL, WANOTIFIER_INTERVIEW_REMINDER_{EN,HI}_URL and WANOTIFIER_JOINING_DETAILS_{EN,HI}_URL secrets. No endpoint or credential belongs in the frontend or repository. Confirm Notification API per-recipient wamid support and provider retry behavior, complete a controlled test, and only then verify templates/sender freshness and enable recruitment updates. Worker requires WANOTIFIER_AUTOMATION_ENABLED, WANOTIFIER_NOTIFICATION_RECEIPTS_VERIFIED, WANOTIFIER_RECRUITMENT_UPDATES_ENABLED and pinned sender identity. Inbound response processing separately requires WANOTIFIER_INTERVIEW_RESPONSES_ENABLED. Keep legacy interview notifications off to avoid two notices.

The existing scheduler script supports recruitment_updates; its production workflow remains installation-locked until provider contract and controlled live tests pass. Unlocking requires reviewed source, correct default branch, immutable source SHA, worker credential and explicit scheduler flags. Delivery reporting uses canonical receipt states; sent is not delivered. Provider status reconciliation remains dependent on a supported provider status contract.
