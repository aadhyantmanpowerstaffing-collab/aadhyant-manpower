Employer guidance

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to submit a requirement?

No. The Quick Requirement form is available without an account.

When should I create a Company account?

Use a Company account when you need recurring, structured requirement creation and tracking.

Does a public requirement create a Company account?

No. Quick Requirement submission and Company registration are separate flows.

Can I track recurring requirements?

Approved Company users can create and manage structured requirements through the existing Company Portal.
